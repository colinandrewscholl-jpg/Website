About the Club

The Pupillometry Club is a workshop series introducing the theory, collection, preprocessing, and analysis of pupil-size data, with a focus on task-evoked pupillary responses (TEPRs) in language comprehension. Materials below are organized by session; everything is also available in the shared folder.
